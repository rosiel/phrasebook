J vs G

J and G are represented by Dots-1245 (⠛）

J

ji as in 几. jia as in 家. jiao as in 叫. jie as in 姐. jiu as in 九. jian as in 见. jin as in 今. jiang as in 讲. jing as in 经. jiong as in 窘.

G

ga as in 嘎. ge as in 个. gai as in 该. gei as in 给. gao as in 高. gou as in 狗. gan as in 敢. gen as in 根. gang as in 港. geng as in 更. gong as in 工. gu as in 古. gua as in 挂. guo as in 国. guai as in 怪. gui as in 鬼. guan as in 关. gun as in 滚. guang as in 光.
